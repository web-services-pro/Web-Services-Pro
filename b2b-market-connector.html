Implementation Framework

How Signal-Based B2B Matchmaking Works

The same four-stage pipeline we use to run our live connector markets, applied to your niche.

01

Signal Mining & Detection

We pull directly from public and government sources to spot businesses showing a provable, active need.

02

Verification & Fit Scoring

Every raw signal is cross-checked against the criteria we agree on with you upfront and tagged by confidence tier — verified or best-effort — so nothing gets delivered on a guess.

03

Access Activation

Once we've verified a real match on both sides, each party activates access before any names are exchanged — supply-side partners through a monthly partnership fee, demand-side businesses through a one-time access fee. No introduction happens before this step.

04

Warm Introduction & Replacement Guarantee

Only after access is activated do we make the introduction — backed by a signed non-circumvention agreement, so neither side can cut us out and go direct. Supply-side partners stay in rotation for future matches; any match that isn't a fit is replaced at no extra cost.

🎯 Fit & Eligibility

Who is the B2B Market Connector service designed for?
It's built for supply-side vendors selling into a specific contractor or trade vertical — staffing and labor agencies, public adjusters, SBA-approved lenders, safety compliance consultants, and specialty suppliers — who need a steady flow of buyers already showing signs of active need.
What industries and markets does Web Services Pro currently cover?
Live matchmaking lanes currently run across roofing (multi-state, including labor and financing partners) and landscaping (labor, storm, and compliance signals), plus a cross-industry OSHA compliance lane. Custom two-sided market buildouts are available for other verticals on request.

🔗 How Matching Works

How do you find businesses that are actively in-market before they start searching?
We track public and government data feeds that signal active need in real time — newly issued contractor licenses, DOL H-2B labor filings, federal contract awards, NOAA storm events, FEMA disaster declarations, and OSHA Failure-to-Abate violations, among others — instead of relying on a static, aging contact list.
What counts as a qualified connection?
A qualified connection is a business that meets the demand-side signal criteria we agree on with you upfront, has been verified as still active, and has activated access to be introduced. Every result is tagged verified or best-effort, so you always know exactly what you're paying for.
How is this different from buying a generic B2B contact list?
A purchased list is static, unqualified, and often six to twelve months stale by the time you use it. Our connector is continuously refreshed against live public filings, verified against your fit criteria before delivery, and comes with a warm introduction instead of a cold contact record.

🔒 Exclusivity & Trust

Are the connections exclusive, or could a competitor buy the same introduction?
Every connection is 100% exclusive to you within your chosen niche and territory. We do not resell the same match to a competing vendor, and every engagement is backed by a signed non-circumvention agreement.
Do you send outreach on our behalf without our sign-off?
No. As with every campaign we run, you approve messaging and targeting criteria before anything goes out under your name or offer.

💰 Pricing & ROI

How much does the B2B Market Connector service cost?
Pricing is structured differently by side. Supply-side partners pay a monthly partnership access fee plus a performance success fee on deals that close. Demand-side businesses pay a one-time access fee to unlock a curated introduction. Exact figures are quoted based on your niche and deal size during the qualification call.
Do I pay before or after the introduction is made?
Before. Access is activated — and payment made — on both sides before any names are exchanged. This keeps every introduction genuinely exclusive and filters out anyone who isn't seriously ready to move forward.
What is a non-circumvention agreement, and why do you require one?
It's a signed clause preventing either side from bypassing Web Services Pro to deal directly with an introduced party in order to avoid the fee, both during the engagement and for 12 months after it ends. It protects the ongoing curation and vetting work that makes the introduction worth paying for in the first place.
How fast can you build out a new two-sided market for my niche?
Timelines depend on how much public data already exists for your niche, but most new matchmaking lanes are scoped and live within a few weeks, following the same signal-mining and verification process used to build our current matchmaking markets.
